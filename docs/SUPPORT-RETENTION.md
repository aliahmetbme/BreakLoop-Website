# Support email retention — draft operational decision

Owner: Ali Ahmet Erdoğdu. Mailbox: breakloopSupport@icloud.com. Service: Apple iCloud Mail.

**Target:** retain a support request for six calendar months after it is closed. This has not been implemented, legally reviewed or verified as operational. It is not a current deletion promise. No backend, database or scheduler has been added.

## Simple proposed manual procedure

1. Test mailbox receiving, replying and deleting on iCloud Mail and any synchronized mail clients. Verify provider trash recovery, deletion behavior and any device backups before making commitments.
2. When a request is resolved, move its thread to a dedicated Closed Support folder. Record the closure date using the chosen mailbox filing convention; document it consistently. Do not treat the last unrelated message as the closure date. A reopened request gets a new closure date only after it is resolved again.
3. During a monthly manual review, identify requests whose closure date is at least six calendar months old. Check any applicable legal obligation, dispute or documented need before deletion. The actual period and exceptions require appropriate legal review.
4. Delete the eligible message thread and its attachments; review synchronized clients and independently saved diagnostic exports. Follow the verified provider trash/deletion process. Do not claim removal of Apple-managed backups or recipients’ copies outside the developer’s control.
5. Keep only a minimal, non-content completion note if needed to verify that the review occurred; define its necessity and retention first. Do not create a second indefinite archive of messages or diagnostic data.

## Before this can become policy

- Confirm lawful purposes, required exceptions, request/identity-verification process and the six-month period’s suitability.
- Actually adopt the folder/date convention, review responsibility and manual routine.
- Perform a real deletion test and establish what iCloud Mail, synced devices, downloads and backups do and do not delete.
- Then update EN/TR notices to accurately describe the operational procedure. Record the actual document date; remove draft language only after completion, not to satisfy a build check.
